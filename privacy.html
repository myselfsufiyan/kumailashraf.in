Privacy Policy

How information is handled on kumailashraf.in

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled on kumailashraf.in, an informational biography website dedicated to the life and legacy of Shaykh Syed Kumail Ashraf.

Information We Do Not Collect

This website does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or login credentials.

Analytics & Usage Data

We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the site. The data collected may include:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Device type and browser
  • Approximate geographic location (city/country level)

This data is collected anonymously and is used solely for improving the website's performance and content.

Cookies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Contact Form

If you submit a message through the contact form, your name, email address, and message content are sent to the website maintainer via Formspree. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties. Formspree's own privacy policy governs how they handle data transmission.

External Links

This website contains links to external platforms including Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

WhatsApp Communication

If you choose to contact the page maintainer via WhatsApp, your communication is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. This website does not store or process those messages.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS (SSL) and hosted on GitHub Pages, helping ensure secure transmission of data.

Consent

By using this website, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
